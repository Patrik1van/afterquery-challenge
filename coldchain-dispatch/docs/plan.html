What we're building, in one minute

An RL environment where an AI agent runs a refrigerated delivery fleet for one shift.

The task. A handful of vans, ~12–40 orders. Each order has a delivery window, a size, and a spoilage deadline. About half the orders only appear mid-shift. The agent plans routes, advances the clock, and has to replan as new orders land.

Why agents struggle. Strong models are good at one-shot puzzles. Here a plan that looks fine now quietly dooms orders 40 minutes later, and a new order means tearing up routes that are already moving. It hits three of AfterQuery's prompts at once:

AfterQuery promptWhere it shows up
Planning several steps aheadRoute order decides which deliveries spoil later
Adapting when new information arrivesNew orders are released during the shift
Managing limited resourcesVan capacity forces 10-minute depot reloads

Why it's a good RL environment. Everything the agent needs is visible; the difficulty is purely in the decisions. The reward is exact (no LLM judge), and a simple reference dispatcher already scores far higher than a myopic one, so there's clear room to learn.
