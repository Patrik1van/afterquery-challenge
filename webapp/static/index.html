🔬 Details for physicists & accelerator operators ▶

CERN TT20 Transfer Line: Extracts 400 GeV/c slow-extracted protons from the Super Proton Synchrotron (SPS) to North Area fixed targets over 900 meters.

Beam Optics & Measurements: Linear uncoupled FODO optics with 10 horizontal / 12 vertical corrector dipoles and 11 horizontal / 10 vertical Secondary Emission Monitors (SEMs). Each SEM reports noisy foil ratios $r = I_1 / (I_1 + I_2)$ and intensity levels $\ell = (I_1 + I_2) / I_{\max}$ under uniform background noise $\nu = 0.25$. Collision checks are evaluated on checkpoints along rectangular vacuum chambers.

Algorithm & Reference: Based on Adam Štafa's CERN report (2026). The Bayesian reference runs a 100,000-particle filter with roughening 0.2 and Thompson-posterior-mean action interpolation.
